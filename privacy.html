Privacy Policy for ThumbnailLab

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At ThumbnailLab, we believe your creative assets belong to you. Because our tool operates entirely on the client side (in your web browser), your images are never uploaded to our servers, stored in a database, or shared with third parties.

2. Information Collection & Usage

  • No Image Uploads: When you use our "Thumbnail Preview" or "Safe-Zone Checker" features, all image processing occurs locally on your own device. The images you upload never leave your browser’s local environment.
  • Log Data: We do not collect personal data, IP addresses, or device information. Your usage of this tool remains anonymous.
  • Cookies: We do not use tracking cookies to monitor your behavior across other websites.

3. Third-Party Advertisements

ThumbnailLab displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdSense, Adsterra). These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the Internet. You can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your files, there is no risk of your thumbnails being intercepted or data-mined by our team. Once you close your browser tab, your session and the images you uploaded are cleared from your browser’s memory.

5. Children’s Privacy

ThumbnailLab is intended for use by content creators of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. CCPA & GDPR Data Protection Rights

Even though ThumbnailLab does not collect, store, or process any personal data or images on servers (as everything runs locally in your browser), we fully support user privacy rights under GDPR and CCPA regulations. No user data is ever sold or shared.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
